Our Story

8finity 🤍

Their story began with a call-out during a CMU Palaro meeting—she stood firm, he walked out annoyed, and neither of them expected that moment to mean anything more. But one message changed everything. What started as arguments turned into conversations, then into something soft, real, and lasting. From strangers to enemies, and eventually to lovers—eight years later, they now choose forever together. And now, with hearts full and hands intertwined, they invite you to witness the moment their once unexpected story becomes a lifelong promise—sealed with love, faith, and a beautiful “I do.” ♾️✨
